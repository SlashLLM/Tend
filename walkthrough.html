Why this path, and nothing else

Tend has ~20 screens across 4 roles. These seven moments carry the entire value story — everything else is depth for people who ask follow-up questions.

✓ Show (the vital few)

  • Educator asks to be observed, and chooses her own focus (agency, not surveillance)
  • Leader still decides when, or declines, so who gets observed stays fair
  • Leader observes live, with AI drafting from evidence — same day, not weeks later
  • Tone Guard catching a harsh word in real time (trust, not just AI)
  • Evidence-linked feedback the teacher can act on, not just read
  • Leader's Insights rolling individual moments into a centre-wide pattern

✗ Skip (the trivial many)

  • Admin: account creation, password resets, framework upload
  • Full Metrics/CSV export, Registry, Programmes browsing
  • Settings, locale switching, accessibility toggles
  • Every one of the 46 QTT indicators — reference just one
  • Offline-vs-Luna pipeline internals, AI run logging

0 Before you start

Two minutes of setup now saves every awkward pause later.

  • Run locally: pnpm db:start && pnpm db:reset && pnpm dev → http://localhost:3000. On a hosted Supabase project instead of Docker, apply the latest migrations with ALLOW_REMOTE_SEED=1 pnpm db:remote migrate (Act 1 needs migration 0005_observation_requests).
  • Confirm DEMO_MODE=1 in .env.local so the persona switcher appears on sign-in. With DEMO_MODE=0 the login page only shows the email and password form.
  • Start from a clean demo world. Running the story changes the data: Ravi can ask only once (the request is then fulfilled), and Aisha's observation moves on after Act 3. Reset before each demo (pnpm db:reset, or pnpm db:remote reset on a hosted test project, which wipes it).
  • No OPENAI_API_KEY? That's fine — offline mode still shows drafting, tagging, Tone Guard and coaching live. Drafts are just labelled "Drafted offline." Don't apologize for this on stage — say it's "Singapore-data-residency-safe mode" if asked.
  • Pre-open two browser windows side by side if presenting solo: one for Sandra, one for Aisha — makes the "same day, two people" beat land without fumbling logins.
Why this matters: the single biggest demo-killer is losing 90 seconds to environment setup mid-story. Do it before anyone's watching.
The story begins

1 Ravi asks to be observed

URL: /login → /home (Ravi), then /today → /observe?request=… (Sandra)

Click, in order
  1. On /login, click the row "Ravi Kumar   N2 Teacher · first year". You land on /home.
  2. The top card reads "Ready for feedback?" with the heading "Ask your leader to observe you". (It only appears when the educator has nothing scheduled, which is why Ravi is used here and not Aisha.)
  3. Click the box "Anything they should know? (optional)" and type a short note, for example Story time on Thursday, trying open questions. A counter shows 0/300.
  4. Click "Request an observation". A toast says "Request sent to your centre leader", and the card turns into "Requested · Your leader has your request", quoting the note, with a "Withdraw request" button.
  5. Sign out (bottom-left of the nav), return to /login and click "Sandra Tan   Centre Leader · Punggol". You land on Today.
  6. The first row in "Needs you" is "Ravi Kumar · Asked to be observed · “Story time on Thursday…”" with an amber hand icon and "Pick a time" on the right. Click it. This opens /observe?request=….
  7. On the Schedule form, an amber note reads "Ravi Kumar asked to be observed" and repeats the note. The Educator field is locked to Ravi. Next to the note is a "Decline request" button (don't click it in the main story).
  8. Fill "When (Singapore time)" with any future date and time, leave the rest, and click "Schedule". A toast says "Scheduled for Ravi Kumar — they’ll choose a focus", and the Upcoming list now shows "Ravi Kumar · Story time · waiting for focus".
"Until now, only the leader could start an observation. Ravi, a first-year teacher, asks for one himself, with a note about what he's trying. Sandra still decides when, so she keeps the calendar and who gets observed stays fair. Her queue lit up the moment he asked."
If asked "what if the leader is too busy?": click Decline request. The educator is notified and can ask again later. They can also withdraw their own request.
Why this is one of the 20%: it shows the educator starting the loop, not only receiving it, without handing over control. One extra screen, and it answers "is this just surveillance?" before anyone asks.

2 Aisha chooses her focus

URL: /login → URL: /home

Click, in order
  1. On /login, find the card titled "Demo: sign in as". It's a list of buttons, each showing a name on the left and a role on the right.
  2. Click the row that reads "Aisha binte Rahman   K1 Preschool Teacher". No password needed — this signs you in instantly and redirects to /home.
  3. On the Home page, the "Coming up" card shows the story time Sandra already scheduled. Click its button "Choose what I'd like feedback on" (a Target icon).
  4. This navigates to a real page, /focus/[id] — not a popup. Pick the indicator shown there (demo data is pre-seeded to 3.3.4). Save your selection.
  5. You're taken back to /home; the button now reads "Change my focus" — your proof the pick registered.
Say

"Normally feedback happens to a teacher. Here, Aisha decides what she wants it to be about. She picks indicator 3.3.4 herself, and Sandra will see that before she walks in."

Why this is one of the 20%: the teacher chooses what the feedback is about, which keeps the product a growth tool owned by the teacher, not a surveillance tool for leaders. Keep it to about 20 seconds.

3 Sandra observes, live

URL: /login → URL: /observe → URL: /observe/[id]

Click, in order
  1. Sign out of Aisha (or open a second browser/incognito window). On /login, click "Sandra Tan   Centre Leader, Punggol".
  2. In the left nav, click "Observe". Page header literally says "Observe"; subtitle: "Short, focused micro-observations. The educator chooses the focus; you capture, Tend drafts."
  3. In the upcoming list, click the card "Aisha binte Rahman · K1 story time". This opens /observe/[id].
  4. At the top of that page is a card labelled "Pre‑brief" — point at it: it shows Aisha's chosen indicator tagged "Chosen focus" and a "Look for" list. This is not a popup, it's always on this page.
  5. Click the large circular Play button (labelled "Start observation"). Helper text under it: "Start when you're ready. Dictate quietly; type a note any time."
  6. Click "Play demo dictation (synthetic)" — only visible before you've captured anything. This auto-plays scripted lines about Aisha's story time so you don't have to talk over yourself.
  7. While it plays, click the round amber ★ star button ("Mark a moment") once — a line "★ Moment" appears in the transcript in bold amber.
  8. Click "End observation" (the green primary button with a check icon at the bottom). It shows "Saving and drafting…" for a moment, then redirects you straight to the Review screen.
"Sandra sees exactly what Aisha asked for before she walks in — that's the pre-brief card. She starts, flags one moment with a single star tap, and ends it. Seconds, not a form."
If asked about real audio: "Needs the OPENAI_API_KEY live; same flow either way" — one sentence, then move on.

4 From evidence to feedback — with a trust check built in

URL: /reviews/[id] (you land here automatically after "End observation")

Click, in order
  1. You're on the Review workspace. Evidence items sit in a left/side column, each with a timestamp chip — click one chip to seek/play that moment.
  2. The draft is grouped under headings "Glows" (up to 3), "Grows" (up to 2), and "Next step" — each line is an editable text box, not static text.
  3. Click into any "Grows" text box and type the word failed anywhere in the sentence.
  4. Watch it trigger immediately as you type (no save needed): a warning appears — "Tone Guard: 'failed' can feel judgemental." — with a button "Use: '…rewritten text…'". Click that button to accept the rewrite.
  5. Scroll to the bottom and click "Approve & share with Aisha". (This button is disabled/greyed out until every line is clean, cited, and within the word limit — point that out.)
  6. Footer line above the button reads: "Nothing reaches Aisha until you approve. It will be labelled 'Drafted with AI · reviewed by Sandra Tan.'" — read it aloud.
"The draft isn't generic praise — it's cited and evidence-linked. And if I try to write something harsh — watch — Tone Guard catches it, live, as I type."
Highest-leverage moment in the demo: answers "is the AI making things up?" (cited), "is this punitive?" (names redacted), and "can this say something cruel?" (Tone Guard, live) in under 30 seconds.
Same day — the loop closes

5 Aisha grows — not just reads

URL: /home → URL: /feedback/[id] → URL: /coach

Click, in order
  1. Switch back to Aisha's session, go to /home. A new card now reads "New growth notes · K1 story time", subtext "From Sandra Tan. Take a moment to reflect first." Click it.
  2. You land on a gate screen, heading "Before you read them: how do you think it went?" — a required textarea (placeholder: "e.g. The children were quiet when I asked why…").
  3. Type at least a few words, then click "Save reflection and read my notes" (stays disabled below ~3 characters — type enough to unlock it).
  4. The feedback unlocks: Glows, then each Grow with buttons "I agree" and "Add context" (click "Add context" to reveal a textarea + Send/Cancel), then the Next step card.
  5. At the bottom, click the 👍 ("Helpful") icon button under "Were these notes helpful?"
  6. In the left nav, click "Coach" (/coach) and type something like "my K1s go quiet when I ask open questions" into the chat to show a live coaching reply.
"Same day — not next term's appraisal cycle. And notice the order: she reflects before she even sees the feedback. That's closed-loop growth, not a PDF in an inbox."
Why it's in the 20%: this is the half every observation tool skips — what happens *after* feedback is given.

6 The debrief writes itself

URL: /reviews → URL: /reviews/[id]

Click, in order
  1. Back in Sandra's session, click "Reviews" in the nav. Header "Reviews"; the list is grouped under "Waiting for your review", "Shared · waiting for the educator", and "Ready to debrief".
  2. Click the observation under "Ready to debrief" (it moves there once Aisha has acknowledged the feedback).
  3. You land on the Debrief panel — point at the numbered agenda (celebrate / questions / decision), built from Aisha's own reflection text.
  4. Click "Start 15-minute debrief" — a countdown timer starts at 15:00.
"Sandra doesn't walk in guessing what to talk about — this agenda came from what Aisha herself reflected on."

7 Zoom out — one teacher becomes a pattern

URL: /insights → URL: /network (as Daniel)

Click, in order
  1. Still as Sandra, click "Centre Insights" in the nav. Title "Centre Insights", subtitle "Where the centre is growing together — built only from approved evidence. Levels, never scores."
  2. Point at the heatmap section, "Heatmap · educators × subdomains" — click any cell to drill into that subdomain's view.
  3. Sign out, go to /login, click "Daniel Koh   HQ L&D".
  4. Click "Network" in the nav (/network) — show the same kind of pattern rolled up across centres.
"Everything you just watched happen to one teacher rolls up automatically — Sandra sees her centre's pattern, Daniel sees the whole network, with nobody filling in a spreadsheet."
Why it's in the 20%: the only moment that proves ROI beyond one happy teacher.
